Project Overview & Problem Statement

Gamifying Shared Finances:
The Grand Line Ledger

An interactive group expense tracking and debt-minimization engine that replaces awkward social debt chasing with collaborative voyage storytelling.

⚡

The Real-World Problem

  • Messy Shared Expenses: Group trips, hackathons, and flatshares lead to scattered bills, forgotten payments, and fractured chat threads.
  • Awkward Debt Collection: Pestering friends for small splits creates psychological tension and social friction.
  • Circular Debt Inefficiencies: In a group where A owes B, B owes C, and C owes A, members execute redundant transactions that rack up bank transfer fees.
  • Boring Spreadsheets: Tools like Splitwise have become paywalled, complex, and uninspiring to maintain.
🧭

Our Proposed Solution

  • Gamified Voyage UX: Shared finances are reframed into an exhilarating pirate voyage where debts are treated as "crew settlements".
  • Algorithmic Debt Minimization: A greedy two-pointer graph engine consolidates circular debts into the minimum possible pairwise settlements.
  • Zero Barrier to Entry: 100% client-side privacy, instantaneous local persistence, and no mandatory registration.
  • Complete Financial Transparency: One-click CSV voyage manifests, category expense metrics, and printable receipts.

  • Motion (Framer Motion): Hardware-accelerated spring animations for fluid carousel and scene transitions.
🧠

Debt Minimization Algorithm

Greedy Two-Pointer Settlement Algorithm running in O(N log N):

1. Calculate Net Balances: B[i] = Paid[i] - Owed[i]
2. Partition into Debtors (B < 0) & Creditors (B > 0)
3. Greedily match max debtor with max creditor
4. Settle min(|Debtor|, Creditor) & advance pointers
Up to 60%
Fewer Bank Transfers Required
