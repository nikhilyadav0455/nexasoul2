Project Overview & Problem Statement

Gamifying Shared Finances:
The Grand Line Ledger

An interactive group expense tracking and debt-minimization engine that replaces awkward social debt chasing with collaborative anime voyage storytelling.

⚡

The Real-World Problem

  • Messy Shared Expenses: Group travel, hackathons, and flatshares lead to scattered bills, forgotten payments, and fractured chat threads.
  • Social Friction & Awkwardness: Chasing friends for small debts creates psychological dread and peer tension.
  • Circular Debt Inefficiencies: Redundant multi-party debts incur unnecessary banking steps and transaction fees.
  • Uninspired Tools: Traditional spreadsheets and corporate trackers are tedious, paywalled, and cumbersome.
🧭

Our Proposed Solution

  • Gamified Voyage Adventure: Turning shared expenses into "crew settlements" creates an engaging, stress-free social experience.
  • Debt Minimization Engine: Consolidates circular liabilities into minimal pairwise transactions in O(N log N) time.
  • Zero Setup Friction: 100% on-device privacy, local persistence, and no compulsory account sign-ups.

Frontend Framework

  • Next.js 16 (App Router): Turbopack compilation delivering sub-second hot reload and production builds.
  • React 19 & TypeScript: Strict compile-time type safety for all financial calculation pipelines.
  • Motion (Framer Motion): Hardware-accelerated spring animations for fluid carousel and scene transitions.
🧠

Debt Minimization Algorithm

Greedy Two-Pointer Settlement Algorithm running in O(N log N):

1. Calculate Net Balances: B[i] = Paid[i] - Owed[i]
2. Partition into Debtors (B < 0) & Creditors (B > 0)
3. Greedily match max debtor with max creditor
4. Settle min(|Debtor|, Creditor) & advance pointers
Up to 60%
Fewer Bank Transfers Required
